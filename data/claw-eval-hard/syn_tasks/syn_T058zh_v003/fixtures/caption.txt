This image displays a segmented, horizontal narrative handscroll in ink and light colour on silk, depicting a bustling urban riverside scene, presented in stacked horizontal sections. It is the supreme monument of Chinese genre painting and urban topography from the Northern Song capital.

1. The Rural Outskirts (Opening)
The scroll opens with quiet suburbs: willows, a stream with a small bridge, donkey trains and porters carrying loads toward the city gate. Two pack animals rest by a wayside structure, and the mood is still rural and calm.

2. The City (Middle Sections)
Inside the walls the scene intensifies into a dense commercial district:
- Shops line both sides of the street, their banners and signboards visible; one establishment advertises wine ("酒") and another a first-class tavern ("正店") with ornate multi-storey architecture, red eaves and hanging lanterns.
- Crowds of pedestrians, sedan chairs, ox-carts, horse-drawn wagons and even a small camel train mingle in the roadway.
- Craftsmen, hawkers, fortune-tellers, diners and officials in differing dress fill the lanes; a tall gatehouse with a raised foundation dominates one section.

3. The River and the Great Bridge (Climax)
A wooden rainbow arch bridge crowded with sightseers and vendors spans the busy waterway. Beneath it a large grain boat is steering through, its crew straining on poles and rigging while onlookers shout directions from the railings. Downstream, moored cargo vessels, sweeping sailboats and ferry craft pack the quay, with granaries and hostels along the bank.

4. Style
Figures are minuscule yet individually characterised — dozens of occupations distinguishable by gesture and costume. Architecture is drawn with ruled precision; the perspective shifts naturally as the scroll unrolls. Ink outlines with pale colour washes dominate.

Overall Impression
The scroll is an encyclopaedic portrait of commercial life at its pre-modern peak: thousands of figures, hundreds of buildings, dozens of boats, all woven into a single continuous panorama of a thriving metropolis during a spring festival season. It is universally celebrated as the greatest genre scroll in Chinese art history.
